How to Delete Your Instagram Account Permanently (2026 Guide)

September 14, 2026

Instagram holds years of memories — photos, videos, and moments. But sometimes, you decide to leave for good. Before you do, know this: permanently deleting Instagram has consequences you can't undo. Including losing your username forever. This guide walks you through the process — safely.

Deactivate or Delete? Know the Difference

  • Deactivate: Account hidden temporarily, data retained, can be reactivated anytime.
  • Delete permanently: Account and all data erased forever. Cannot be recovered.

What You Lose When You Delete Instagram

  • All photos and videos: Gone, not recoverable
  • Followers and following: All connections lost
  • DMs and story archives: All erased
  • Username may be taken: Your handle is not guaranteed to be available again. Someone else could claim it
  • Business accounts lose access: Connected Facebook Pages and Instagram Shopping are disconnected

Steps to Delete Instagram Account Permanently

Step 1: Open Accounts Center

Open Instagram, tap More in the bottom left, select Settings, then Accounts Center.

Step 2: Select Personal Details

Tap Personal Details, then Account Ownership and Control.

Step 3: Choose Deactivation or Deletion

Tap Deactivation or Deletion, then select the account you want to delete.

Step 4: Choose Delete Account

Tap Delete Account, then Continue.

Step 5: Verify and Confirm

Instagram will ask for re-verification. Complete the confirmation steps to schedule permanent deletion.

Instagram's Grace Period

After you request deletion, Instagram applies a period before data is fully erased. During this time, do not log back in if you truly want it gone.

Yes. After the deletion period ends, data cannot be recovered.
