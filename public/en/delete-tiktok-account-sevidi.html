How to Delete Your TikTok Account Permanently (2026 Guide)

September 14, 2026

Deleting your TikTok account isn't as simple as tapping a button. There's a grace period, data consequences, and risks most people don't know about. Whether you're doing a digital detox, worried about privacy, or cleaning up an old account, this guide will walk you through how to delete your TikTok account permanently — the safe way.

Why People Delete Their TikTok Accounts

  • Digital detox: Reducing screen time and endless scrolling
  • Privacy concerns: Worried about how data is used
  • Old accounts: Accounts no longer in use but still active
  • Memorial accounts: Deleting an account of a deceased loved one
  • Past content: Removing old or embarrassing videos

What Happens When You Delete TikTok

Before you start, understand what you'll lose:

  • Permanent loss of access: You can never log in again
  • All videos deleted: Uploaded videos, drafts, and favorites are gone
  • Coins and balance forfeited: Withdraw your balance first
  • Username available again: Someone else could claim your handle

Step-by-Step: How to Delete TikTok Account

Step 1: Open Your Profile

Log in to the TikTok app on the account you want to delete. Tap the Profile icon in the bottom right corner.

Step 2: Go to Settings and Privacy

Tap the three-line menu icon in the top right, then select Settings and Privacy.

Step 3: Select Account

Tap Account to manage your personal data.

Step 4: Tap "Deactivate or Delete Account"

Scroll to the bottom of the Account menu and tap Deactivate or Delete Account.

Step 5: Choose a Reason

TikTok will ask why you're leaving. You can select a reason or tap Skip.

Step 6: Download Your Data (Recommended)

TikTok offers to download your data first. If you have important videos, do this before continuing.

Step 7: Verify Your Identity

Enter your password or a verification code sent to your email or phone. This confirms the request is from the account owner.

Step 8: Confirm Deletion

Once verified, your account will be deactivated and enter a 30-day grace period. If you don't log in during that time, the account is permanently deleted.

The 30-Day Grace Period: Your Second Chance

TikTok gives you 30 days before permanent deletion. During this period, your account is only deactivated. If you change your mind, simply log back in with your old credentials and tap Reactivate. All your videos, drafts, and followers return as they were.

The Hidden Data Risk Most People Ignore

This is the part most people miss. Many turn to third-party account deletion services because they can't access their own account. But government agencies — including Indonesia's Ministry of Communication and Information (Kominfo) — have issued official warnings:

"Providing personal data to third parties can lead to data misuse — including identity theft, fraud, and unauthorized access to other social media accounts."

Irresponsible deletion services often ask for a photo of your ID card. This data is at high risk of being stolen or misused.
