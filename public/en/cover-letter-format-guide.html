Cover Letter Format: The Complete Guide for 2026

October 22, 2026

The best cover letter content in the world won't matter if the format is a mess. Recruiters judge your professionalism by how your cover letter looks—before they even read a word.

In this guide, I'll show you the exact cover letter format that employers and ATS systems expect in 2026.

What Recruiters Look For in Cover Letter Format

  • ✅ Clean, professional layout
  • ✅ Easy to scan (short paragraphs, clear headings)
  • ✅ Consistent font and spacing
  • ✅ Proper contact information
  • ✅ Correct sign-off

Cover Letter Format (2026 Standard)

1. Contact Information (Top of Letter)

Include your full name, email, phone number, LinkedIn URL, and location (city, state).

Example:

      Sarah Chen
      sarah.chen@email.com
      (555) 123-4567
      linkedin.com/in/sarahchen
      New York, NY

2. Date

Write the full date below your contact info, before the hiring manager's details.

Example: October 22, 2026

3. Hiring Manager's Contact Information

Include their name, title, company name, and address if you have it.

Example:

      Ms. Jessica Rodriguez
      Marketing Director
      BrightWave
      123 Main Street
      New York, NY 10001

4. Salutation

Use "Dear [Name]," or "Dear Hiring Manager," if you don't know the name.

Example: "Dear Ms. Rodriguez,"

5. Body (3-5 Paragraphs)

Structure your content into short, scannable paragraphs:

  • Paragraph 1: Hook + role you're applying for
  • Paragraph 2: Your relevant experience and achievements
  • Paragraph 3: Why you're interested in this company
  • Paragraph 4: Your value proposition
  • Paragraph 5: Closing + call to action

6. Sign-Off

End with a professional sign-off and your name.

Example:

      Sincerely,
      Sarah Chen

Standard Cover Letter Format Specs (2026)

Element Specification
Length 1 page (200-350 words)
Font Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman
Font size 10-12 pt
Margins 1 inch on all sides
Spacing Single-spaced with blank lines between paragraphs
Alignment Left-aligned (not justified)
File format PDF (unless specified otherwise)
File name YourName-CoverLetter-Company.pdf

Sarah Chen
sarah.chen@email.com

Ms. Jessica Rodriguez

Dear Ms. Rodriguez,

I'm excited to apply for the Marketing Manager position at BrightWave. With 6 years of experience in digital marketing, I'm confident I can drive growth for your team.

At BlueSky Media, I led campaigns that increased organic traffic by 80% and generated 2,000+ qualified leads per quarter.

I admire BrightWave's innovative approach to consumer engagement and commitment to sustainability.

Thank you for your consideration. I look forward to discussing how I can contribute to your team.

Sincerely,
Sarah Chen

Email Cover Letter Format (Alternative)

If you're sending your cover letter via email, use this simpler format:

Subject: Application for Marketing Manager – Sarah Chen

Dear Ms. Rodriguez,

Body paragraphs (3-4 short paragraphs)

Best regards,
Sarah Chen
sarah.chen@email.com
(555) 123-4567

Common Format Mistakes to Avoid

  • ❌ Using a table or columns (ATS can't read them)
  • ❌ Overly colorful fonts or backgrounds
  • ❌ No spacing between paragraphs
  • ❌ Using "To Whom It May Concern" when you can find a name
  • ❌ Sending as a Word doc when they expect PDF
  • ❌ Including a photo (not standard in the US)

ATS-Friendly Formatting Tips

  • ✅ Use standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman)
  • ✅ Avoid images, graphics, or tables
  • ✅ Use standard section headings
  • ✅ Save as PDF (unless they ask for Word)
  • ✅ Use a simple, single-column layout

Final Thoughts

Perfect content deserves the perfect format. Follow these cover letter format guidelines to ensure your letter looks as professional as your skills are impressive.

FAQ

1. What is the best cover letter format for 2026?

A single-page, left-aligned letter with 1-inch margins, using a standard font like Arial or Calibri (10-12pt).

2. Should I use a template or write from scratch?

Using a template is fine as long as you customize it with your own experience and the specific company.

3. What font is best for a cover letter?

Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman are the most professional and widely accepted fonts.

4. Should I save my cover letter as a PDF or Word?

PDF is safest because it preserves formatting. Only use Word if the company specifically asks for it.

5. How many paragraphs should a cover letter have?

3-5 paragraphs is standard. Keep each paragraph focused and concise.
