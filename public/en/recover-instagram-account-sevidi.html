How to Recover a Hacked or Lost Instagram Account (2026 Guide)

September 14, 2026

Your Instagram account suddenly won't let you in? Password changed without your knowledge? Or the account got blocked for no clear reason? Don't panic. There are official steps you can take — and times when you should get professional help.

This guide walks you through how to recover a hacked or lost Instagram account using the official process, plus when it's time to call in a specialist.

Common Reasons Instagram Accounts Get Lost

  • Hacked: Password and email changed by an unauthorized person
  • Blocked: Violated Instagram's community guidelines
  • Deactivated: Flagged for suspicious activity
  • Forgotten login: No memory of password, email, or phone

Step 1: Check if the Account Still Exists

  1. Ask a friend to search your username on Instagram.
  2. If it appears — the account exists, but you just can't log in.
  3. If it doesn't appear — it may have been deleted or permanently deactivated.

Step 2: Try Alternative Login Methods

A. Log In with Old Email or Phone

If you remember an old email or phone number, try logging in with it. Make sure the phone number format is correct (with country code).

B. Use "Forgot Password"

On the login screen, tap Forgot password?. Enter your username, email, or phone. Instagram will send a login link.

C. Check Your Email for Instagram Alerts

If your account was hacked, the hacker may have changed your email. Check your inbox for any Instagram notification about an email change. If found, click "Secure my account" in that message.

Step 3: Report a Hacked Account

  1. Visit help.instagram.com
  2. Select Privacy & Safety
  3. Choose Report Something → Hacked Account
  4. Select "I think my account has been hacked"
  5. Click Report it to us
  6. Instagram will email you instructions
  7. Complete the verification steps
  8. Verify identity via email, phone, or previously used device
  9. Wait for recovery (usually a few days)

Step 4: Recover a Blocked Account

  1. Open Instagram and try to log in
  2. Instagram will notify you that the account is blocked
  3. Choose the available recovery option
  4. Follow the appeal instructions
  5. Wait for the review team

Step 5: Contact Instagram Help Center

  1. Go to help.instagram.com
  2. Fill in all requested details
  3. Explain your issue clearly
  4. Provide a clear reason for the problem
  5. Submit the form
  6. Wait for resolution (usually a few days)

When You Need Professional Help

  • No access to registered email/phone: If the hacker changed all contact info, verification becomes very hard
  • All official steps failed: Instagram isn't responding or denies your request
  • High-value business account: Losing access can be very costly
  • Urgent recovery needed: For business or work purposes

A Warning About Third-Party Recovery Services

Regulators have warned that handing personal data to unverified third parties can lead to misuse — fraud, identity theft, or hacking of other accounts. Choose a service that is transparent and doesn't ask for unnecessary sensitive data.
