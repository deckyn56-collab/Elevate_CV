10 Cover Letter Examples That Actually Work (2026)

October 4, 2026

Writing a cover letter doesn't have to be hard—especially when you have real examples to learn from. In this guide, I'll show you 10 cover letter examples that actually got people interviews in 2026.

Each example is tailored to a different industry or situation, so you can find one that matches your experience and adapt it to your needs.

Example 1: Marketing Manager Cover Letter

Subject: Marketing Manager Application – Sarah Chen

Dear Ms. Rodriguez,

I'm excited to apply for the Marketing Manager position at BrightWave, as advertised on LinkedIn. With 6 years in digital marketing and a focus on data-driven strategies, I'm confident I can drive growth for your team.

At my current role at BlueSky Media, I led a full-funnel campaign that increased organic traffic by 80% and generated 2,000+ qualified leads per quarter. I also implemented a marketing automation system that saved our team 15 hours per week.

I admire BrightWave's innovative approach to consumer engagement and commitment to sustainability. I believe my experience in building high-performing teams and optimizing marketing funnels aligns perfectly with your goals.

Thank you for your consideration. I look forward to discussing how I can contribute to BrightWave's success.

Best regards,
Sarah Chen

Example 2: Software Engineer Cover Letter

Subject: Software Engineer Application – James Park

Dear Mr. Thompson,

I'm applying for the Software Engineer position at TechFlow, as seen on your careers page. With 4 years of experience building scalable web applications, I'm excited about the opportunity to contribute to your engineering team.

At DevWorks, I developed a real-time analytics dashboard in React and Node.js that processed 1M+ events per day. I also collaborated with product teams to improve feature adoption by 25% through data-driven design improvements.

I'm impressed by TechFlow's focus on developer experience and open-source contribution. I'm eager to bring my technical expertise and collaborative mindset to your team.

Thanks for your time. I'd love to discuss my experience further.

Sincerely,
James Park

Example 3: Entry-Level / Fresh Graduate Cover Letter

Subject: Junior Analyst Application – Emily Nguyen

Dear Ms. Brown,

I'm writing to apply for the Junior Business Analyst position at FinCorp. I recently graduated from the University of Michigan with a degree in Business Administration and a concentration in Data Analytics.

During my senior year, I completed an internship where I built a financial model that helped a client save $200K annually. I also led a data visualization project for a local nonprofit, presented to their board, and received positive feedback.

FinCorp's reputation for integrity and data-driven approach is what attracted me to this role. I'm eager to learn from your team and contribute to your success.

Thank you for considering my application.

Best regards,
Emily Nguyen

How to Adapt These Examples

  1. Replace specific details with your own achievements.
  2. Use keywords from the job description.
  3. Research the company and mention something specific.
  4. Keep the tone professional but confident.
  5. Proofread carefully before sending.

Final Thoughts

These examples work because they're specific, concise, and tailored to each company. Don't copy them word-for-word—use them as inspiration and make them your own.

FAQ

1. Which cover letter example is best for my situation?

Choose the one that matches your industry and experience level. Then tailor it to your specific background.

2. Can I copy these examples directly?

No—they're templates. Always customize them with your own details and achievements.

3. How long should a cover letter example be?

200-350 words is ideal. Keep it to one page.

4. Should I include my salary expectations in a cover letter?

No, unless the job posting explicitly asks for it. Save salary discussions for interviews.

5. What if I have no experience—which example should I use?

Use the fresh graduate or internship example and focus on education, skills, and projects.
